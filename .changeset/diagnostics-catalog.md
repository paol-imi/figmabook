The diagnostics registry now holds every code of the plan's catalog (12 §6.2), generated by `pnpm diag:gen`. `DiagnosticCode` is the union of catalog codes. New: `diagnostic(code)`, `isDiagnosticCode`, `DIAGNOSTIC_CATALOG`, `DIAGNOSTIC_CODES`, `DIAGNOSTIC_EMITTERS`, `DIAGNOSTIC_DOMAIN_TITLES`, `MESSAGE_TEMPLATES` and the `CatalogDiagnosticDef` type. Registered definitions carry `emittedBy`, `definedIn` and, where the catalog has one, `severityNote`. `defineDiagnostic` rejects codes that are not in the catalog.
